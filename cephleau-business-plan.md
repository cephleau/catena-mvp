# Cephleau Consulting — Business Plan

## Executive Summary
Cephleau Process Improvement Consulting is a systems and operations optimization firm focused on improving efficiency, throughput, and performance in complex industrial environments. It integrates process engineering, systems thinking, and data governance to deliver measurable operational improvements.

**Founded by:** Senior Project Manager at Boeing with Master of Science in Computer Science (pursuing MBA)

---

## Target Market

### Primary Clients
- Aerospace and defense organizations
- Manufacturing companies
- Logistics and supply chain operations
- Large enterprise transformation teams

### Market Position
Industrial systems optimization and operational intelligence consultancy specializing in aerospace and manufacturing environments.

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Build Credibility (Months 1-3)
- Develop 2-3 case studies from Boeing experience (with NDA compliance)
- Publish thought leadership on LinkedIn (process improvement, systems thinking)
- Offer pilot projects at reduced rates to build portfolio
- Target industry-specific forums and conferences

### Phase 2: Small Engagements (Months 4-6)
- Launch diagnostic offerings ($2.5-7.5K projects)
- Build relationships with procurement and operations leaders
- Create industry-focused content (e.g., "5 Common Manufacturing Bottlenecks")
- Network within aerospace/defense supplier ecosystem

### Phase 3: Scale to Enterprise (Months 7-12)
- Transition successful pilots into larger engagements
- Develop retainer relationships for ongoing advisory
- Build strategic partnerships with implementation firms
- Target Fortune 500 transformation initiatives
