## STEP 2: Upload Your File (2 minutes)

### Option A: Drag & Drop (Easiest)
1. On netlify.com, look for **"Drag & drop here"** area or click **"New site"**
2. Drag `cephleau-uiux-pro.html` from Finder
3. Netlify auto-deploys
4. You get a temp URL like `abc123.netlify.app`
5. Test it works ✓

### Option B: Web Upload
1. Click "Create new site"
2. Click "Upload an existing project"
3. Select `cephleau-uiux-pro.html`
4. Deploy

---

## STEP 3: Connect Your Domain (2 minutes)

1. In Netlify dashboard, go to **Site settings**
2. Click **Domain management**
3. Add your domain: `cephleau.com`
4. Netlify shows you 4 nameservers
5. Note them down

Example Netlify nameservers:
```
dns1.p06.nsone.net
dns2.p06.nsone.net
dns3.p06.nsone.net
dns4.p06.nsone.net
```

---

## STEP 4: Update Your Domain (2 minutes)

### Your Domain Registrar
You registered `cephleau.com` (likely via Cloudflare, GoDaddy, or Namecheap)

1. Log into your registrar
2. Find **Nameservers** settings
3. Replace with Netlify's 4 nameservers (from Step 3)
4. Save changes
5. Wait 5–15 minutes for DNS to propagate

---

## STEP 5: Test It Works! (1 minute)

1. Open browser
2. Go to **https://cephleau.com**
3. See your website live? ✅ YOU'RE DONE

If not loading yet:
- Wait 15 minutes (DNS propagation)
- Clear browser cache (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac)
- Check nameservers are correct

## NEED HELP?

### Netlify account? 
Go to netlify.com → Sign up (free)

### Can't find drag-drop?
Click "Create new site" → "Upload"

### Nameservers not working?
- Double-check you entered them correctly
- Wait another 10 minutes
- Try incognito window (clear cache)
- Check DNS propagation: mxtoolbox.com/DNSLookup

### Website looks broken?
- Press Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) to hard-refresh
- Open F12 → Console → check for errors
- File is complete and tested — should work!
