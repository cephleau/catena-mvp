## 1. Services

**1.1 Scope of Services.** The Contractor agrees to provide professional interpretation services ("Services") as requested by the Company on an on-demand, assignment-by-assignment basis. Services may include:

- **In-Person Interpretation:** On-site interpretation at client-designated locations
- **Telephonic Interpretation (OPI):** Over-the-phone interpretation via Company-designated systems
- **Video Remote Interpretation (VRI):** Interpretation via video conferencing platforms designated by the Company

The Contractor will confirm acceptance or notify the Company of unavailability within **[X] hours** of receiving the assignment notification.

---

## 2. Independent Contractor Status

**2.1 No Employment Relationship.** The Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment relationship between the Parties.

**2.2 Contractor Control.** The Contractor retains full discretion over the manner and means of performing interpretation, provided the Services meet the quality standards set forth in Section 7. The Company may specify the outcome and scope of assignments but shall not control the method of interpretation.

**2.3 No Benefits.** The Contractor is not entitled to, and shall not receive, any employee benefits from the Company, including but not limited to health insurance, retirement plans, paid time off, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance.

**2.4 Tax Responsibility.** The Contractor is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on compensation received under this Agreement, including self-employment taxes. The Company will issue a Form 1099-NEC for any calendar year in which payments to the Contractor equal or exceed the IRS reporting threshold. The Contractor shall provide a completed IRS Form W-9 prior to the first payment.

**2.5 Tools and Equipment.** For telephonic and VRI assignments, the Contractor is responsible for maintaining adequate equipment (phone, computer, headset, internet connection) to perform services professionally. For in-person assignments, the Contractor is responsible for their own transportation.

---

## 3. Assignment Acceptance and Rejection

**3.1 Voluntary Assignments.** The Contractor is under no obligation to accept any particular assignment. The Company is under no obligation to offer any minimum number of assignments.

**3.2 Acceptance Process.** When the Company offers an assignment, the Contractor shall respond within the timeframe specified in the offer. Failure to respond within that window may result in the assignment being offered to another interpreter.

**3.3 Right to Decline.** The Contractor may decline any assignment for any reason without penalty, subject to the cancellation provisions in Section 8.

**3.4 No Exclusivity.** The Contractor may provide interpretation services to other clients or companies, provided doing so does not conflict with the confidentiality or non-solicitation obligations in this Agreement.

---

| Assignment Type | Rate |

## 5. Confidentiality

**5.1 Confidential Information.** In the course of providing Services, the Contractor may have access to sensitive and privileged information, including but not limited to:

- Client identities, personal details, and case information
- Medical, legal, social services, or immigration records and proceedings
- Company business information, pricing, client lists, and contracts

**5.2 Non-Disclosure.** The Contractor agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose, share, or use such information for any purpose other than fulfilling the assigned interpretation services. This obligation applies during and after the term of this Agreement.

**5.3 Minimum Necessary Standard.** The Contractor shall not retain, copy, photograph, or record any portion of an interpreted session without explicit written authorization from the Company.

**5.4 Compliance with Law.** The Contractor acknowledges that certain assignments may involve HIPAA-protected health information, attorney-client privileged communications, or other legally protected content. The Contractor agrees to comply with all applicable privacy laws and professional standards of interpreter confidentiality.

**5.5 Survival.** The obligations of this Section 5 survive termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

---

## 6. Non-Solicitation

**6.1 Client Non-Solicitation.** During the term of this Agreement and for a period of **twelve (12) months** following its termination or expiration, the Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly:

- Solicit, contact, or accept independent interpretation work from any client of Catena Language Solutions that the Contractor became aware of through the Company; or
- Encourage any client of the Company to reduce or discontinue its relationship with the Company.

**6.2 Scope.** For purposes of this section, "client" means any organization, agency, business, or individual for whom the Company has arranged or performed interpretation services at any time during the Contractor's engagement with the Company.

**6.3 Reasonableness.** The Parties acknowledge that this restriction is reasonable in scope and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests. If any provision of this section is found unenforceable, a court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

---

## 7. Quality Standards and Professionalism

**7.1 Professional Conduct.** The Contractor agrees to:

- Arrive on time (or log in on time) for all accepted assignments; punctuality is defined as being ready to begin **[10] minutes** prior to the scheduled start time
- Dress professionally and appropriately for the setting (for in-person assignments)
- Maintain impartiality and neutrality; interpret accurately and completely without omission, addition, or editorializing
- Disclose to the Company any actual or potential conflict of interest prior to accepting an assignment
- Immediately notify the Company if a technical issue arises during a VRI or telephonic session that may impact service quality

**7.2 Certifications and Qualifications.** The Contractor represents that they possess the language proficiency, training, and (where applicable) certifications required to perform the Services. If the Contractor holds professional certifications (e.g., CMI, CCHI, court interpreter certification), they shall notify the Company and maintain such credentials in good standing.

**7.3 Code of Ethics.** The Contractor agrees to adhere to the National Council on Interpreting in Health Care (NCIHC) Code of Ethics, the Registry of Interpreters for the Deaf (RID) standards (if applicable), or any other recognized professional interpreting code relevant to the assignment context.

**7.4 Feedback and Improvement.** The Company may provide feedback on interpreter performance. The Contractor agrees to consider such feedback in good faith as part of maintaining service quality.

---

## 8. Cancellation Policy

**8.1 Contractor Cancellation.** If the Contractor accepts an assignment and subsequently needs to cancel, the Contractor shall notify the Company as soon as possible and no later than:

| Notice Period | Compensation |
|---|---|
| More than **48 hours** before assignment | No penalty; no compensation |
| **24–48 hours** before assignment | No compensation; one (1) documented late cancellation noted |
| Less than **24 hours** before assignment | No compensation; may affect future assignment offers |
| **No-show** (no notice given) | No compensation; grounds for immediate termination of Agreement |

**8.2 Company Cancellation.** If the Company cancels an assignment after the Contractor has confirmed acceptance:

| Notice Period | Compensation to Contractor |
|---|---|
| More than **48 hours** before assignment | No compensation |
| **24–48 hours** before assignment | $\_\_\_\_ flat fee or \_\_% of assignment value |
| Less than **24 hours** before assignment | $\_\_\_\_ flat fee or \_\_% of assignment value |
| **Same day / after arrival** | Full minimum fee or agreed hourly minimum |

**8.3 Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be penalized for cancellations resulting from circumstances beyond their reasonable control, including severe weather, medical emergencies, or natural disasters, provided notice is given as promptly as possible.

## 9. Liability and Indemnification

**9.1 Contractor Indemnification.** The Contractor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Company, its officers, managers, employees, and agents from and against any claims, damages, losses, or expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to:

- The Contractor's negligent, reckless, or intentional acts or omissions in performing Services;
- The Contractor's breach of any representation, warranty, or obligation under this Agreement; or
- The Contractor's violation of any applicable law or professional standard.

**9.2 Company Indemnification.** The Company shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Contractor from and against claims arising out of the Company's own negligence, willful misconduct, or material breach of this Agreement.

**9.3 Limitation of Liability.** To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages arising out of or related to this Agreement.

---

## 10. Term and Termination

**10.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either Party.

**10.2 Termination Without Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time with **[14] days' written notice** to the other Party.

**10.3 Termination With Cause.** The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the Contractor:

- Fails to maintain confidentiality or violates Section 5;
- Engages in unprofessional, discriminatory, or harassing conduct toward clients or Company staff;
- No-shows for an accepted assignment without prior notice;
- Misrepresents credentials or qualifications; or
- Materially breaches any provision of this Agreement.

**10.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, the Contractor shall promptly return or destroy any Company materials or client information in their possession. Sections 5 (Confidentiality), 6 (Non-Solicitation), 9 (Liability), and 11 (General Provisions) shall survive termination.

## 11. General Provisions

**11.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Colorado, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**11.2 Dispute Resolution.** The Parties agree to attempt to resolve any dispute informally before pursuing formal legal action. If informal resolution fails, disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Denver, Colorado, under the rules of the American Arbitration Association, unless both Parties agree otherwise.

**11.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, or representations.

**11.4 Amendments.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written document signed by both Parties.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**11.6 Waiver.** Failure by either Party to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of future enforcement of that provision.

**11.7 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by email or mail to the addresses on file for each Party.
