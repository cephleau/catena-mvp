## Colorado vs. Wyoming LLC — Recommendation

**Use Colorado for both. Here's why:**

| Factor | Colorado | Wyoming |
|---|---|---|
| Filing fee | $50/LLC | $100/LLC |
| Annual report | $10/yr | $60/yr minimum |
| Where you operate | Colorado | Colorado (foreign registration required = extra cost) |
| Privacy | Standard | Better (no public member names) |
| Complexity | Simple | Requires foreign registration to operate in CO |

**Verdict:** Wyoming only makes sense if privacy or asset protection is a top priority. For two service businesses operating in Denver, Colorado LLCs are cheaper, simpler, and avoid the foreign registration hassle. Go Colorado.

## Colorado LLC Formation — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Confirm Name Availability
- Go to: https://www.coloradosos.gov/ → Business Search
- Search "Cephleau Consulting" and "Catena Language Solutions"
- Name must be distinguishable from existing CO businesses
- Must include LLC designator (e.g., "LLC" or "L.L.C.")

### Step 2 — Appoint a Registered Agent
- Must have a **physical Colorado address** and be available during business hours
- Options:
  - **You** (free, but you must be at that address 9-5 M-F)
  - **Northwest Registered Agent** (~$125/yr) — recommended for privacy + convenience
  - **ZenBusiness, Registered Agent Inc.** — similar pricing
- As of July 2025: agent must formally consent before being listed

### Step 3 — File Articles of Organization
- File online at: https://www.coloradosos.gov/
- **Fee: $50 per LLC** (online only, processed in real-time)
- You'll need: LLC name, registered agent name + address, principal office address, your name as organizer

### Step 4 — Create Operating Agreement
- Not required by Colorado law, but **strongly recommended**
- Establishes: ownership %, profit distribution, decision-making rules, what happens if you exit
- For a single-member LLC, keep it simple — a 1-2 page template is fine
- I can draft one for each business on request

### Step 5 — Get Your EIN (Federal Tax ID)
- Apply free at: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online
- Takes ~10 minutes, issued immediately
- You'll need this to open a business bank account and issue 1099s to contractors
- Apply for **each LLC separately** — each gets its own EIN

### Step 6 — Open Business Bank Accounts
- Recommended: **Relay** (free, no minimums, great for small business) or **Mercury**
- One account per LLC — keeps finances clean for taxes and liability protection
- Don't mix personal and business funds

### Step 7 — Licenses & Permits
- Colorado has no general state business license
- Denver may require a **Denver Business License** (~$50/yr) — check: https://denvergov.org/business
- **Catena Language Solutions:** No state license required for language services agencies (interpreters themselves may need certifications, not the agency)
- **Cephleau Consulting:** No special license required for management/process consulting

## Notes
- Colorado LLCs require an **annual Periodic Report** — $10/yr, due in your anniversary month
- No need for a separate state tax registration unless you're selling taxable goods (services are generally not subject to CO sales tax)
- Keep your LLC in good standing: don't miss the annual report or you risk "Delinquent" status
